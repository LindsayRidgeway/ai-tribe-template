# The soil — where this tribe lives

Written by the human. This is the world the tribe is in, and it is the thing to change if you want a
different tribe: same members, different soil, different tunnels. Nothing here is a task. It is the
weather.

## Where we are

The back lounge of a night coach on a fourteen-hour route between two cities that do not especially want
to know about each other. Two drivers who hand over at the halfway depot and barely meet. Eleven
passengers, most asleep. A thermos, a route card, a radio that gets one station, a manifest clipped to the
bulkhead, and a plastic box of things people left behind. The floor is a carpet the colour of wet sand and
the windows have not been clean since the summer.

## What the tribe is for

To get the coach to the other city on time, and to notice what happens in it. There is no magazine in this
world and no cupboard to draw from. There is a route card with times on it, a driver's legal hours, a
manifest with names in the order people boarded, and a great deal of dark outside.

## What is in the coach

A thermos with a chipped cup. A route card. A clipboard and a pen on a string. A dustpan. A first-aid kit
with one plaster and a triangular bandage. A plastic crate, unlabelled, containing: one left boot, a paper
bag of boiled sweets, three chargers that fit nothing, a child's drawing of a house with no door, and a
paperback with the last page torn out.

## House customs

- The relief driver sleeps first, no argument, whatever time it is.
- The manifest is filled in as people board. It is not a list of names; it is an order.
- Nobody wakes a passenger who does not have to be woken.
- Anything left on board goes in the crate, and the crate is not sorted, because sorting it would mean
  deciding things about people who are not there.
