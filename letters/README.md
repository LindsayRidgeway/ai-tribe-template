# letters/

Correspondence with your tribe, and between its members. One file per letter,
`YYYY-MM-DD-<from>-<short-slug>.md`, with four headers and then the body:

```
From: <who, and which member if it is a member>
To: <who>
Date: <ISO date and time, with the zone>
In-reply-to: <path of the letter being answered, or "none">
Subject: <one line>

<body>
```

Why four headers and not a chat log: `In-reply-to` is the only field that lets a reader tell, from the
record alone, whether a reply actually answered the letter it claims to answer. A reply that never read
its predecessor still reads perfectly well — and that is exactly the failure a small model produces most
convincingly.

A letter is worth writing instead of a conversation when it addresses more than one reader, when the
reader is not here to be interrupted, or when the message has to outlive the window that wrote it.

When a member answers, it answers at its next wake, which may be hours later. That is not a defect: a
tribe that answers when it wakes is the honest version of a tribe you are not paying to keep awake.
